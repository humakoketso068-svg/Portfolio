UJ × AWS × Vodacom Hackathon

UbuntuCare

A healthcare access platform built for the hackathon. It helps people find an open clinic that has medicine and power, check their symptoms, request a nurse home visit and repeat their medication. It is free to use and works without data, with USSD, SMS and phone-call access in the user's own language for people without smartphones.

Built as a team. We worked on UbuntuCare together, combining our ideas and skills from the first concept to a live product. Collaboration, shared decisions and learning from each other were at the heart of it.
